# Group assignment 2.3: Can’tilever Stop the Beat

As a warmup you will create and analyze some elementary signals yourself, and next, you will carry out frequency domain analyses on a given data-set, namely acceleration measurements of a Cantilever-Beam experiment.

Most of the Tasks in the notebook consist of both coding, producing a plot, and answering (open) questions. Typically, as you work your way through the Tasks, you can often re-use code, or part of it, from earlier tasks. That will save you a lot of work!!
